# Hyperlocal Marketplace Research & Competitive Benchmarks

## Research Insights & Core NEARVIA Differentiator

- **Hyperlocal 5 km Focus**: Unlike broad job boards (Apna, WorkIndia, Job Hai) that focus on general employment or full-time staffing across entire metropolitan areas, NEARVIA is laser-focused on immediate physical proximity (~5 km) where workers can commute within minutes.
- **Short-Duration Work Priority**: Tailored specifically for micro-tasks (1-2h), short shifts (3-6h), and same-day emergency staff cover rather than multi-week hiring funnels.
- **Explainable Match Scoring**: Replaces opaque algorithmic ranking with auditable, multi-factor transparent scores (Skill + Distance + Availability + Reliability).
- **Assisted Access**: Leverages a trusted local agent model to onboard low-digital-literacy workers without isolating them from the digital economy.
